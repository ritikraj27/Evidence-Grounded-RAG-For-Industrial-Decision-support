# Maintainability

Ease of maintaining a functioning product or service

**Maintainability** is the inherent design characteristic of a system or product that determines the probability that it can be retained in, or restored to, a specified operating condition within a given time when [maintenance](/wiki/Maintenance "Maintenance") is performed under prescribed conditions using specified procedures and resources.[[1]](#cite_note-1)

## Usage in different fields

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&veaction=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Usage in different fields") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Usage in different fields")]

### Engineering

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Engineering") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Engineering")]

In [engineering](/wiki/Engineering "Engineering"), maintainability is the ease with which a product can be maintained to:

- correct defects or their cause,
- [Repair](/wiki/Repairability "Repairability") or replace faulty or worn-out components without having to replace still working parts,
- prevent unexpected working conditions,
- maximize a product's useful life,
- maximize efficiency, reliability, and safety,
- meet new [requirements](/wiki/Requirements "Requirements"),
- make future maintenance easier, or
- cope with a changing environment.

In some cases, maintainability involves a system of [continuous improvement](/wiki/Continuous_improvement "Continuous improvement") - learning from the past to improve the ability to maintain systems, or improve the reliability of systems based on maintenance experience.

### Telecommunication

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&veaction=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Telecommunication") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Maintainability&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Telecommunication")]

In [telecommunications](/wiki/Telecommunications "Telecommunications") and several other engineering fields, the term maintainability has the following meanings:

- A characteristic of design and installation, expressed as the probability that an item will be retained in or restored to a specified condition within a given period of [time](/wiki/Time "Time"), when the [maintenance](/wiki/Repair_and_maintenance "Repair and maintenance") is performed by prescribed procedures and resources.
- The ease with which maintenance of a [functional unit](/wiki/Functional_unit "Functional unit") can be performed by prescribed requirements.
